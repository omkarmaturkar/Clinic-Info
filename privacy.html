Privacy Policy

Mahalakshmi Dental Clinic app – last updated October 2026.

What the app stores

Patient details, treatments, payments, appointments and clinical photos entered by the clinic. This information is stored on the clinic's own device.

Google Drive backup

If the clinic turns on Google Drive backup, a backup copy is saved to the clinic's own Google Drive. The app uses Google's "drive.file" permission, so it can only see and manage the backup files it creates itself. It cannot read any other files in the Google Drive.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or send any information to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

Contact

For any questions, please contact Mahalakshmi Dental Clinic directly.
